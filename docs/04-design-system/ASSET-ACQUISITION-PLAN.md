# ProSmash — Asset Acquisition Plan

> Status: Proposed for owner approval · Last updated: 2026-11-14
> Scope: what imagery to acquire, at what quality, and where it lives.
> Nothing is downloaded or generated now; previews use labelled placeholder
> frames per the component-library rule (never fake products as photography).
> Folder authority: `docs/ASSET-INVENTORY.md` (approved) — this plan fills it.

## 1. Acquisition Priorities (in order)

| # | Asset set | Blocks | Qty |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Homepage hero photograph(s) | Homepage high-fidelity sign-off | 1–3 |
| P2 | 12 category images | Category tiles/strip upgrade to photographic | 12 |
| P3 | Product photography — launch catalogue | Catalog + product-detail pages with real SKUs | per SKU |
| P4 | Supplier/manufacturing context imagery | "For suppliers" journey, About content | 4–6 |
| P5 | Promotional banner frames | Campaign slots (post-launch) | as needed |

## 2. Sourcing Routes

**Recommended: a commissioned one-day shoot** at a partner school/academy
(hero + category contexts in one session) plus **tabletop product photography**
batched as the launch catalogue is finalized. Commissioned work gives clean,
India-authentic imagery with full usage rights — the Court Ink photography
treatment (VISUAL-IDENTITY §7) depends on controlling light and context.

**Acceptable interim: licensed stock** for hero/category only (never for
product pages), on these conditions:
- License permits commercial web use; perpetual or clearly tracked term.
- Indian or context-neutral settings; no identifiable third-party branding
  on kit (swoosh-covered gear is a legal and brand problem).
- Model releases confirmed where faces are prominent.

**Product photography is never stock** — every product image must show the
actual SKU sold. Supplier-provided imagery is acceptable if quality rules
(§4) are met and written usage permission is recorded.

**Prohibited:** AI-generated product or athlete imagery presented as real;
watermarked comps; imagery whose license cannot be documented.

## 3. Shot Lists

- **Hero (P1):** academy/school training in progress — net practice, court
  drills; dusk/indoor directional light; subject right-weighted so the 16°
  crop and ink overlay (55–70%) carry display type on the left. Portrait 4:5
  crop for the split hero, 3:2 master.
- **Category (P2):** one strong image per sport — equipment-in-context over
  posed athletes (e.g. a bat and ball on turf for cricket, shuttles on court
  for badminton). Consistent palette temperature across all 12.
- **Product (P3):** per SKU — 1 hero angle + 2–3 detail angles + 1 scale/
  in-hand shot where meaningful. Chalk or light-grey seamless background,
  consistent camera height per category (ASSET-INVENTORY convention).
- **Supplier context (P4):** workshop/warehouse craft — hands, materials,
  stringing, stitching; anonymised (no third-party brand marks).

## 4. Technical & Quality Requirements

| Use | Aspect | Minimum master | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homepage hero | 3:2 master (4:5 crop safe) | 2560 px wide | JPG/WebP, full-res master retained |
| Category image | 4:3 | 1600 px wide | JPG/WebP |
| Product hero/detail | 4:3 | 1600 px wide | JPG/WebP on seamless |
| Product thumbnail | 1:1 (derived crop) | 400 px | generated at build |
| Promo banners | per slot | 2560 px wide | JPG/WebP |

Quality bar: sharp focus on subject, true colour (neutral grey-balanced),
no heavy filters — the only treatment ever applied is the flat ink overlay,
added in build, never baked into masters. Masters are kept uncompressed;
optimized variants are generated by the (future) build pipeline — never
hand-edit exports (ASSET-INVENTORY rule 3).

## 5. Folder Organisation & Naming (per approved ASSET-INVENTORY.md)

```
assets/banners/homepage/   home-hero-{slug}-{WxH}.jpg      ← P1
assets/categories/         cat-{sport-slug}.jpg             ← P2 (12 files, exact slugs)
assets/products/<sport>/<product-slug>/
                           {slug}-{angle}-{nn}.jpg          ← P3 (hero-01, detail-02…)
assets/suppliers/          sup-context-{nn}.jpg             ← P4 (anonymised context)
assets/banners/promotional/ promo-{campaign}-{slug}.jpg     ← P5
```

**Licensing record (mandatory):** each asset directory receiving third-party
imagery gets a `LICENSES.md` listing per file: source, author/agency, license
type, license/invoice reference, date acquired, permitted use. Commissioned
work records the agreement reference and rights granted. No file enters
`assets/` without its license line — same rule as `brand/fonts/LICENSES.md`.

## 6. Placeholder Protocol (until acquisition)

All previews use the Court Ink placeholder frame (hatched `neutral.100`,
mono caption naming the target path and ratio, e.g.
`IMG · cricket/eng-willow-bat-g2 · 4:3`). The caption is the acquisition
work-order: every placeholder names exactly the file that replaces it.

## 7. Open Points for the Owner

1. Budget route: commissioned shoot (recommended) vs licensed stock interim
   for P1/P2.
2. A partner institution willing to host the hero/category shoot.
3. Launch-catalogue SKU list (drives P3 volume) — depends on initial
   procurement decisions.
4. Who signs off imagery against the brand treatment (suggest: owner +
   design lead review against VISUAL-IDENTITY §7).
