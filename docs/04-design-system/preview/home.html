Process

How ProSmash works

STEP 01

Register and get approved

Your institution's details are reviewed before ordering is enabled.

STEP 02

See your price

Standard prices are public. On approval, your institutional price appears on every product, GST-inclusive.

STEP 03

Order with 100% advance

Pay by UPI, card, net banking, NEFT/RTGS or cheque. Orders dispatch after payment is confirmed — no credit risk, for anyone.

STEP 04

One counterparty, end to end

ProSmash manages fulfilment and stays your single point of contact for delivery, end to end.

The ProSmash standard

S·01
Suppliers join by approvalEvery supplier is reviewed and approved by ProSmash before participating in procurement.
