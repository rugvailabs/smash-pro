## 1. Brand Positioning

**ProSmash is the professional trade floor of Indian sport.**

Not a sports shop that added bulk pricing; not a listing directory with a
logo. ProSmash is the managed B2B platform where verified institutions procure
sports equipment with the seriousness their budgets deserve — one
counterparty, negotiated wholesale supply, dependable fulfilment.

Positioning statement:

> For schools, academies, colleges and retailers who buy sports equipment at
> institutional scale, ProSmash is the premium B2B commerce platform that
> turns fragmented, unreliable dealer purchasing into professional
> procurement — because ProSmash verifies every supplier, negotiates every
> price, and stands behind every delivery.

The brand lives at the intersection of two worlds and must honour both:

| From sport | From commerce |
|---|---|
| Energy, movement, ambition | Precision, verification, accountability |
| The athlete's standard: perform | The CFO's standard: deliver |

Most competitors pick one side — consumer-sporty (loud, retail, untrustworthy
at scale) or procurement-corporate (grey, lifeless). ProSmash's distinctive
territory is **disciplined athleticism**: the visual confidence of elite sport
delivered with the composure of a financial institution.

## 2. Brand Personality

Five traits, each with a guardrail so it never tips into cliché:

1. **Confident** — states things plainly, leads with numbers and proof.
   *Never arrogant or hype-driven.*
2. **Precise** — every detail accounted for: specs, quantities, dates, GST.
   *Never bureaucratic or cluttered.*
3. **Energetic** — carries the forward motion of sport in its angles, pace
   and imagery. *Never loud, neon or juvenile.*
4. **Trustworthy** — verification, audit trails and single-point
   accountability are brand features, not fine print. *Never defensive or
   legalistic in tone.*
5. **Institutional** — speaks the language of committees, budgets and
   academic years. *Never stiff; this is sport, after all.*

**Voice:** short declarative sentences. Active verbs. Numbers stated exactly
(₹ with two decimals, dates in full). No exclamation marks in product UI.
Marketing may raise the temperature one notch — never into hype.

## 3. Target Audiences

| Audience | Who decides | What they need to feel |
|---|---|---|
| Schools & colleges | Admin officers, sports teachers, purchase committees | "This is safe to put before the committee." |
| Academies | Owner-coaches, operations managers | "These people know equipment and won't waste my season." |
| Retailers | Proprietors, buyers | "Real wholesale margins, no games." |
| Suppliers (manufacturers → dealers) | Sales heads, proprietors | "Serious demand, fair process, I'll get paid." |
| Internal (Super Admin) | ProSmash operations | "I can run the whole trade loop from here." |

A single brand must earn trust from a school principal and a factory sales
head simultaneously — another reason the identity leans premium-professional
with sporting energy as the accent, not the base.

## 4. Brand Promise

**"Procurement at match standard."**

Every promise the platform makes rolls up to this: verified counterparties,
exact pricing, stock that exists, deliveries that are proven and confirmed.
The elaborated promises per audience:

- To buyers: *One trusted counterparty for all of sport.*
- To suppliers: *Qualified demand, a fair process, transparent settlement.*
- Internally: *Nothing moves without a record.*

## 5. Brand Expression Hierarchy

Where the brand shows up, in order of expressive intensity:

1. **Public website** — fullest expression: imagery, display typography, the
   diagonal graphic language (see VISUAL-IDENTITY.md).
2. **Buyer portal** — professional commerce: brand-warm but
   information-first.
3. **Supplier portal** — workmanlike clarity; brand present in structure and
   type, restrained in decoration.
4. **Super Admin portal** — the quietest surface: brand lives in typography,
   spacing discipline and the accent colour used strictly for action and
   status. Density is the aesthetic.

The rule: **expressiveness decreases as operational density increases** — the
brand never competes with work. The mechanics of this gradient are specified
in PORTAL-PRINCIPLES.md.
